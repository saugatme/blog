Haven't written in a while now. Felt that writing will ease my pain and help me be a better person. Thought it would help, but, it didn't.

Still, writing is something I still like. Hopefully from now on in, I will feel better. Let's hope for the best.

Completed my Bachelor's in Engineering. What started off well, finished well but the thought for future made the final moments very sour. Knew that things wouldn't be much easier, but it's just one setback after the other. Had a torrid time during exams. Still don't know. I try, and I try even harder, yet everything just wrecks. What's the most depressing thing is the fact that I fail despite trying my hardest. It sucks because I've lost all my confidence. The one quality I had that kept me sane, you know. I always knew I had the ability to succeed in anything I tried. But, once you loose your confidence, everything just falls apart. Second guessing yourself constantly, getting worried, having anxiety. It sucks. The confidence I had acted as a mask for me for a long long while. Once the confidence bid farewell, I changed. Everything changed. I didn't need to even try to get good grades, once upon a time. I believed in myself. I knew I was good. The only good thing I was above average was, was learning. Once, I lost my confidence, even my ability to read was gone.

Seeing everyone earn stings. I know I can perform their tasks a lot better than they ever could. But, it's a world of talkers and not doers. You wish you had a good job. Neither  I have the ability to own a transport medium nor a job. People have both. They enjoy day in day out. Earn a good money, money enough to survive. Yet they complain.

It's been a year now, did a job for 2 months. Payment wasn't great but I genuinely didn't care. But, I knew that world wasn't for me. I believed, and I still do that, I deserve something better. Maybe I'm over-estimating myself but, I know that I am capable of much more. I can't be arsed walking for 30 minutes at 12 in the day and push cars for a living. I don't want to go to hospitals and be treated like a nobody. I am a learner, and I have abilities to go beyond. My talent isn't gaslighting hospital authorities or getting involved in useless talks. Anyway, I didn't hate my job. But, despite being treated that way, I was okay because it's part of life . What more can I do. Didn't have a bike. Didn't trust myself on a bike. Had zero confidence in my ability. So, I had to quit. I'd be making good money now.  But, it wasn't meant to be.

Soooo, been learning everyday. Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Language Models and Artificial Intelligence. Buzz words today. Applied for jobs. Didn't get any.  From the money I earned, enrolled in a training center. Liked the experience. Got a certificate anddddd that's all. Again back to zero. From biggest learning platforms, got certifications. Gained skill, knowledge. Delved into a new world. Still, no job. It's my fault too. I applied when I wasn't ready. And, after a year now, I still don't feel ready. Confidence, you know. The fear of failure during interview. That scares me shitless. I can't fail again. I can't afford to fail again.

No one knows what's next. I just talk to moon and stars and count planes. That's my life now. It's peaceful, relaxing. Knowing that people out are enjoying their lives, I'm sat here just existing. Nothing more. Just someone somewhere doing everything to get nothing . That's me. That's the tale of the year gone by.
